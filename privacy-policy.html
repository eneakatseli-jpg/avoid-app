🚨 AVOID - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

AVOID ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data: When you press the ALERT button, we collect your GPS location to notify nearby drivers. Your location is only shared when you actively press the ALERT button.

Account Information: We collect your email address when you create an account.

Device Token: We collect your device's push notification token to send you alerts from nearby drivers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send emergency alerts to drivers within 2km of your location when you press ALERT
  • Receive alerts from other drivers near your location
  • Manage your account and provide customer support

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your location data is only used in real-time to notify nearby drivers and is not stored permanently after the alert expires.

4. Data Retention

Alert data is automatically deleted after 24 hours. Account data is retained until you delete your account.

5. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@avoid-app.com.

6. Location Permissions

AVOID requires location permission to function. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings, but this will prevent the app from working.

7. Push Notifications

We send push notifications to alert you of nearby emergencies. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

8. Security

We use Firebase (Google) to store data securely. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

9. Children's Privacy

AVOID is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
